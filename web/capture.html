Wayfarer Capture Coach

Guides you while you film and checks the video before the long build, until it is good enough for a sharp twin.

The job server isn't reachable. Start everything with start.bat on the PC, and open this page from the address it prints.

Before you start: lights on, fans off, doors open, mirrors and screens covered, printed marker on a wall in the first room. Use the 1x lens and hold the phone sideways.

Photos of the space

Take 8–15 photos: one from each corner looking into the room, each doorway, and one of the ceiling. Leave everything as it is now; the coach will tell you what to change.
