The live demo is the "fast apartment" twin built by the Wayfarer pipeline from the public Redwood indoor apartment RGB-D scan: 3,276,779 Gaussian splats, 38.4 m² walkable floor, 18 automatically labelled points of interest. Walls and furniture block movement (collision against the occupancy grid).
